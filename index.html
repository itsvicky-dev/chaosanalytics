ChaosAnalytics

ChaosAnalytics is a private, self-hosted analytics tool used by the creator of the ChaosKannamma gaming channel to review the performance of their own YouTube and Instagram content and plan future videos.

It runs only on the owner's personal computer. It requests read-only access to the owner's own channel data and never posts, edits or deletes content.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
